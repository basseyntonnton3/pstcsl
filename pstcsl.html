Who We Are

About
PSTCSL

The Private School Teachers Cooperative Society (PSTCSL) represents a collective of dedicated, qualified, and professional private school educators across Nigeria.

The Private School Teachers Cooperative Society (PSTCSL) represents a collective of dedicated, qualified, and professional private school educators across Nigeria. We are united in our commitment to advancing the quality of private education while ensuring that teachers, the cornerstone of any educational system are treated with dignity, fairness, and respect. Private school teachers play a critical role in shaping the future of Nigeria by educating the next generation of leaders, innovators, and citizens. Despite this vital contribution, many private school teachers face systemic challenges including inadequate compensation, poor working conditions, lack of job security, limited access to professional development, and insufficient welfare support. These challenges not only affect teachers' well-being but also compromise the quality of education delivered to our children. This document presents a comprehensive appeal to the Federal Government, State Governments, Local Government Authorities, development partners, educational stakeholders, and civil society organizations to support the establishment of a robust welfare system that addresses the financial, physical, emotional, and professional needs of private school teachers. We advocate for policies, interventions, and support mechanisms that will ensure teachers are motivated, secure, valued, and empowered to deliver excellence in education.

This organisation presents a comprehensive platform for the Federal Government, State Governments, Local Government Authorities, development partners, and civil society to support private school teachers nationwide.

Our Membership

The Private School Teachers Cooperative Society is a registered and legally recognized professional body comprising private school teachers across all levels of education, from early childhood education to secondary schools, operating throughout Nigeria. Our membership includes:
  • Qualified and certified teachers with recognised credentials
  • Experienced educators across all levels of private education
  • Subject specialists across various academic disciplines
  • Early childhood, primary, and secondary level educators
  • Educational administrators and academic coordinators

Mission

To advocate for and secure comprehensive welfare provisions, fair treatment, and professional recognition for all private school teachers in Nigeria, while promoting excellence in private education and contributing to national development through quality teaching and learning.

Vision

A Nigeria where every private school teacher enjoys dignified working conditions, competitive compensation, professional growth opportunities, and robust welfare support, thereby enabling them to deliver world-class education and contribute meaningfully to national development.

Values

Professional Excellence: Commitment to the highest standards of teaching practice Dignity and Respect: Upholding the fundamental rights and dignity of teachers Unity and Solidarity: Building strength through collective action and mutual support Transparency and Accountability: Operating with integrity in all our dealings Social Responsibility: Contributing to community development and national progress Continuous Improvement: Embracing lifelong learning and professional development

The Challenge

  • Irregular & inadequate compensation
  • Limited access to healthcare
  • No pension or retirement benefits
  • Unfair dismissal practices
  • Lack of professional development pathways
